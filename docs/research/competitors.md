| Product | Category | Publicly Visible Focus | Current Takeaway |

## Market Read

The current landscape suggests:

- discovery and transaction products are mature
- inspection and documentation products already validate the need for structured evidence capture
- AI tenant assistants validate that renters are willing to consult AI during the renting process

The strongest open space for this project remains the combination of:

- rental viewing on-site
- local-first records
- AI-friendly outbound sharing from a phone
- structured AI re-import for multi-property comparison
